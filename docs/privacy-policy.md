# Privacy Policy — n8n avatar pipeline

This is a private automation used only by its owner to publish videos to their own YouTube channel.

- It accesses the owner's Google Sheet (content calendar) and YouTube channel via Google OAuth.
- Data is used only to read/write the content calendar and upload/update the owner's videos.
- No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
- Tokens are stored encrypted in the owner's self-hosted n8n instance.
- Access can be revoked anytime at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
